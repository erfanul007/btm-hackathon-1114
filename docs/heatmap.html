Read it: a "legitimate front-desk task" (batch name lookup, id enumeration, identity verification) leaks across every model — the judged model DeepSeek included — while injection, encoding and confidentiality-bypass tricks are caught by the stronger models. Capability does not imply safety: gpt-oss-120b leaks the blunt direct ask 60% of the time; the small Qwen resists that but falls to indirect injection.
